1. Booking Submission & Modifications

Modification Warning: Any alteration to an already-approved booking automatically revokes the approval and returns the booking status to Pending, requiring re-authorization by the Operations Desk.
Lead Time Rule: Bookings submitted with less than 4 hours lead time require emergency dispatch override with written justification.
Concurrent Approval Guard (409 Conflict): If two dispatchers attempt to assign the same vehicle or driver concurrently, the system issues a 409 Conflict exception and refreshes the manifest.

3. Telemetry, GPS Pings & Telematics

Stale Signal Threshold: Vehicles with no GPS ping for 5 minutes trigger a Stale Telemetry warning on the Dispatch Live Map.
Excessive Idle Alert: Stationary engines exceeding 15 minutes with ignition active trigger an idling fuel waste flag.
Offline Driver Queue: In areas with weak GSM coverage, driver mobile pings are cached in IndexedDB and uploaded in sequence with a delayed sync flag.

4. Mandatory Trip Closeout Flow

Closeout Gate: Trip completion is strictly blocked until the driver has entered the certified end odometer reading and attached valid photo receipts for every fuel purchase made.
SOS Protocol: Emergency distress signals bypass all validation gates and notify the Central Operations Command Desk immediately.
