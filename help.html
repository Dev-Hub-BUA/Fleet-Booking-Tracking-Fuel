Roadside Breakdown & Incident Protocol

1
Secure Passengers & Vehicle:

Pull vehicle to the safe hard shoulder, activate hazard flashers, and position emergency warning triangles 50 meters back.

2
Trigger SOS in Driver Run View:

Press the Red SOS button on the Driver Live Run screen. GPS coordinates are automatically broadcast to the Dispatch map hub.

3
Dispatch Relief Vehicle:

The Central Operations Desk will immediately dispatch the nearest available standby vehicle or recovery tow truck.
