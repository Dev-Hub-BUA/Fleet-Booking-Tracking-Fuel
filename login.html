Fleet Operations & Dispatch User Guide

1. Vehicle Booking Request (Requester) Requesters submit mission bookings at least 4 hours in advance with full itinerary and route details; budget is provisionally reserved from the department quota.
2. Review & Dispatch (Dispatcher) The operations desk validates vehicle and driver availability, enforcing turnaround inspection (30 min) and sanitization buffers (45 min).
3. Trip Execution (Driver) Drivers access the mobile web manifest, complete pre-trip safety checklist, and log odometer readings and fuel receipts in real time.
4. Audit & Financial Reconciliation (Auditor) Actual distance and fuel consumption are reconciled against standard allowances. Discrepancies exceeding ±10% trigger compliance flags requiring formal review.
